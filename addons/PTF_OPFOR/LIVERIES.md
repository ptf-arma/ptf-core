# Vehicle liveries — status and workflow

## The approach

Custom liveries are done by **compositing over RHS's own textures**, not by
replacing them. The original `.paa` is decoded to PNG, a markings layer is
drawn on top, and the result is re-encoded. Every panel line, weld seam and
weathering pass RHS painted stays underneath.

An earlier version of this file claimed liveries needed a human artist. That
was wrong — it assumed authoring a camouflage scheme from scratch, which is
indeed not worth attempting. Compositing markings over an existing texture is
a much smaller problem and is fully automated here.

### The pipeline

`ImageToPAA` converts in **both** directions, which is what makes this work:

```
RHS .pbo  --Extract-Pbo.ps1-->  .paa  --ImageToPAA-->  .png
                                                        |
                                          composite markings layer
                                                        v
addons/PTF_OPFOR/data/*.paa  <--ImageToPAA--  .png
```

Generators live in the session scratchpad (`Make-KestrelDecal.ps1`,
`Make-Flags.ps1`) — copy them into `tools/` if these need regenerating often.

## What is done

### Faction differentiation by hull and existing texture set

| Faction | Look | How |
|---|---|---|
| los Pereños | Faded green, ex-Soviet soft-skins beside ex-US tracks | GREF insurgent UAZ/Ural/GAZ + HIDF M113/M1025 |
| La Guardia | Green, heavier, Soviet | GREF insurgent BTR-70 / BMP-2 / UAZ-AGS |
| Kestrel | US woodland pattern | RHS USAF `rhsusf_m1025_w_m2` rather than the HIDF green M1025 |

The desert HMMWV was considered and rejected — Valmera is tropical jungle and
Kestrel are professionals who would not run desert paint through it.

### Kestrel vehicle markings — `data/kestrel_decals_co.paa`

RHS's HMMWV exposes `unitdecals_1` / `unitdecals_2` selections specifically so
markings can be swapped without touching the body paint. Stock, the woodland
M1025 wears **101st Airborne 2/502** markings — plainly wrong on a contractor
vehicle, so this was a correctness fix as much as a livery one.

The replacement sheet carries Kestrel's double chevron, a `K - 04` callsign and
`KESTREL GROUP` text. Marking positions were measured off RHS's own sheet so
they land on the same UV regions:

| Marking | Region | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Chevron (crisp) | x 9–62, y 7–57 | black `0,0,0` |
| Chevron (faded) | x 77–130, y 7–57 | olive `85,92,77` |
| Callsign | x 109–189, y 71–95 | olive |
| Unit text | x 11–224, y 170–196 | black |
| Unit text (faded) | x 11–224, y 217–243 | olive |

The paired tones are RHS's convention — a crisp marking and a sun-faded one,
mapped to different panels. Both are kept.

**Only index 8 of `hiddenSelectionsTextures` changes.** Both arrays are
restated in full on `PTF_Kestrel_m1025_m2` because a partial override leaves
the remaining selections untextured. Note the textures array is 10 entries
against 13 selections — the three indicator selections are driven by the
lighting config, not a texture. This mirrors RHS exactly.

### La Guardia repaint — UAZ and Ural

The UAZ and the Ural were the **only hulls shared between factions**, and stock
they are the identical green: at 300m a Guardia technical and a Pereño
technical were the same vehicle.

RHS's own `ins` / `ins_g` variants are no help — comparing the two class
definitions, they differ *only* by faction, side and crew. Not one texture
changes. That dead end is worth knowing before anyone tries it again.

So La Guardia carry a darker, desaturated repaint generated by
`Recolor-Texture.ps1`: the original texture composited through a GDI+
`ColorMatrix` (saturation 0.40, brightness 0.72, slight cool shift). Nothing is
repainted by hand — every panel line and weathering pass survives, just shifted
in tone. It reads as a regime security force rather than an army, which suits
what La Guardia are. los Pereños stay on stock RHS green.

| Texture | Source | Size |
|---|---|---|
| `guardia_uaz_co.paa` | `uaz_main_001_co.paa` | 1.8 MB |
| `guardia_ural_kabina_co.paa` | `ural_kabina_co.paa` | 0.5 MB |
| `guardia_ural_plachta_co.paa` | `ural_plachta_chdkz_co.paa` | 0.5 MB |

Use a `ColorMatrix` via `DrawImage`, not `GetPixel`/`SetPixel` — these textures
are up to 2048² (4M pixels) and a scripted pixel loop takes minutes each.

### La Guardia hull stencil — BMP-2

`data/guardia_bmp2_hull_co.paa`, generated by `Add-Stencil.ps1`. A
"LA GUARDIA" marking with the faction's crimson bar, composited onto **both
hull flanks** of RHS's own texture at 78% opacity so it sits into the surface
rather than looking like a sticker.

**The camouflage is not repainted at all** — only the stencil pixels change.
This is the right tool where a faction needs branding rather than
disambiguation.

It is also cheap, because a stencil only touches the *one* texture carrying
the hull sides. The BMP-2 has a six-texture set; only `bmp_3` holds the flanks,
so this costs **1.6 MB instead of ~12 MB**, with the other five entries
pointing straight back at RHS.

Placements (source pixels, 2048² texture): `470,1150,400,62` and
`470,1400,400,62` — the two flank panels in the lower half of the atlas.

To find placements on another vehicle, render the decoded texture with a
coordinate grid overlay first (the technique that produced `bmp3_grid.png`),
then read coordinates straight off it.

### Rejected: recolouring the BMP-2 and BTR-70

A full tonal repaint of the armour was tried and deliberately **not shipped**:

1. The first pass turned them **blue** — the tone matrix tuned on the UAZ's
   lighter olive over-cooled an already-dark, saturated source. Retuning kept
   them green but made the change almost invisible.
2. It bought no recognition anyway. Both are Guardia-exclusive, so they are
   already unambiguous by silhouette, and a repaint costs the *whole* texture
   set (~16 MB) versus 1.6 MB for a stencil.

**The rule:** repaint hulls that two factions *share*; stencil hulls that only
one faction fields. Repainting an exclusive vehicle is branding at maximum
cost, and a stencil delivers the same branding for a tenth of the megabytes.

## What is still open

### BTR-70 stencil — needs a UV check first

Not attempted, deliberately. On `btr70_chdkz_1_co.paa` the bulk of the atlas is
the hull viewed from above, and the flanks appear to be the **vertical** strips
either side of the deck — meaning a stencil there must be rotated 90° to read
correctly on the vehicle, and a wrong guess puts sideways text on the hull.

Confirm the orientation in-game (place a BTR-70 and note where a test marking
lands) before drawing one. `Add-Stencil.ps1` would need a rotation parameter.

### RHS's own decal system

Worth knowing before authoring anything: several RHS hulls expose
`rhs_decalParameters` driving `rhs_fnc_decalsInit`, which paints tactical
numbers and door roundels from **Eden attributes, with no texture work at
all**. Confirmed present on the UAZ (`cDecals4CarsNumberPlaces`), the BMP-2
(`cBMP3NumberPlaces`, `cBMPLeftBack`) and the Tigr.

For per-vehicle callsigns and platoon numbers this is strictly better than a
baked texture — it varies per placed vehicle and costs nothing. Use custom
textures for faction identity, and the decal system for individual numbering.


**The GREF vehicles have no decal slots.** The M113, BMP-2, BTR-70, UAZ and
Ural expose `camo1`..`camo5` — the painted body textures themselves. Adding
markings there means compositing directly onto a hull texture rather than into
a dedicated transparent decal sheet.

That is the same pipeline and entirely doable, with one extra step: you must
look at the decoded body texture and find a flat hull panel to place the
marking on, since a badly placed mark lands across a wheel arch or on the
underside. Decode the texture, view it, pick the spot, composite, re-encode.

Priority if someone continues:

1. **La Guardia's BMP-2** — their signature vehicle. Making the campaign's
   nastiest armour recognisable at range is worth real tension.
2. **La Guardia's BTR-70** — same reasoning, lower impact.
3. **los Pereños** — arguably should stay unmarked. Their whole visual
   identity is "nobody has repainted anything in fifteen years", and a crisp
   stencil would undercut it.

### Extracting a texture to work on

```
scratchpad/Extract-Pbo.ps1 -PboPath <rhs.pbo> -OutDir <dir> -Filter '*.paa'
ImageToPAA.exe input.paa output.png      # decode
ImageToPAA.exe output.png input.paa      # re-encode
```

Keep the original dimensions — they must stay powers of two.
